Method 2: USB Cable se Direct Fast Connection (No Wi-Fi/Firewall Needed)
Agar aap apne phone ko USB cable se laptop se connect karte hain:

Laptop Chrome me jayein: chrome://inspect/#devices
"Port forwarding..." button par click karein.
Port 5173 -> localhost:5173 aur 8000 -> localhost:8000 add karein.
Phone ke browser me simply http://localhost:5173 kholein — ultra-fast USB speed par direct khulega!
